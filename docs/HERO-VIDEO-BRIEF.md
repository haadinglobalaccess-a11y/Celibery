# Hero video brief (answer to Q7)

CELIBERY asked for a detailed explanation of the hero video (or an AI-made one). This brief can be handed to a videographer **or** used as the prompt basis for an AI video tool. The website already supports it: paste the video URL in **Admin → Website content → Homepage → Hero video URL**. Until then, the homepage shows hero banners or an animated slider of real catalogue products.

## Format

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Length | 10–15 seconds, seamless loop (last frame ≈ first frame) |
| Aspect | 16:9 master (1920×1080) **plus** a 9:16 mobile crop (1080×1920) — most Saudi visitors are on phones |
| Sound | None — the video autoplays muted |
| File | MP4 (H.264), ≤ 6 MB desktop / ≤ 3 MB mobile; also export a still JPG poster frame |
| Text | **No text burned into the video** — headline and buttons are added by the website in Arabic or English |
| Safe area | Keep the lower-left third (lower-right on Arabic pages) calm and dark: the headline sits there |
| Look | Dark, premium, near-black backgrounds, soft directional light, one accent colour (CELIBERY brand accent) |

## Storyboard (5 shots × ~3 s)

1. **Morning, Riyadh apartment** — a phone on a CELIBERY wall charger on a marble counter; city skyline softly out of focus. Slow push-in.
2. **Car, golden hour on a highway** — hand plugs a CELIBERY car charger in; dashboard glows. Gentle side track.
3. **Desk / work** — laptop and phone charging from one multi-port adapter, coffee cup (dallah/finjan optional). Top-down slide.
4. **Travel / Umrah** — power bank slipping into a travel pouch next to a passport; airport or Makkah-bound bus window light. Close-up.
5. **Product hero** — power bank, cable and charger on a dark plinth, slow 30° rotation, rim light in the accent colour. Loops back to shot 1.

## Casting & culture

- Saudi setting and props; hands-only shots avoid model-release issues and keep focus on products.
- Modest styling; no music or speech required.
- Use only real CELIBERY products and packaging — no claims, numbers or logos of other brands.

## AI-generated option

If an AI tool is used, generate each shot separately with the descriptions above ("photorealistic, cinematic, shallow depth of field, dark premium lighting, Saudi interior"), then composite real product photography for shot 5 so products are accurate. Review every frame for distorted products, text or hands before publishing.
